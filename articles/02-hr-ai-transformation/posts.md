## ISAAC DONNELSON — Personal LinkedIn Post (CMO Voice)

HR teams are drowning in admin.

57% of HR time goes to tasks that don't require human judgment.

Scheduling interviews. Routing PTO requests. Chasing down performance review forms. Sending the same onboarding email for the 200th time.

We built WorkflowFiesta to fix this — not by replacing HR, but by giving HR back the time to do the work that actually matters.

Here's what the 5 highest-ROI HR automations look like in practice:

**1. Recruiting pipeline**
Application received → AI screens and scores → shortlist drafted → interviews scheduled automatically. A 250-resume job post gets processed in minutes, not days.

**2. Onboarding**
Offer accepted → IT provisioning triggered → welcome sequence sent → 30-day check-in scheduled. 8–10 hours of HR time per hire drops to under 2.

**3. PTO management**
Request submitted → policy check runs → manager notified → calendar updated. Same-day approval replaces a 2–3 day cycle.

**4. Performance reviews**
Cycle opens → reminders sent → forms distributed → responses routed → calibration scheduled. 4–6 weeks of coordination overhead cut by 60–70%.

**5. Offboarding**
Resignation received → access revocation checklist triggered → exit survey sent → accounts archived. The security risk of manual offboarding disappears.

The judgment calls — who to hire, how to handle a difficult conversation, whether someone is a culture fit — those stay with your HR team.

The logistics don't have to.
