Most HR teams I talk to are spending 60%+ of their time on admin.

Scheduling interviews. Chasing onboarding approvals. Running the same 40-step checklist for every new hire. Answering the same PTO questions every week.

None of that requires a human. It requires a rule.

The HR teams that are pulling ahead right now have figured out one thing: AI doesn't replace HR. It eliminates the process work so HR can actually do HR.

Here's what that looks like in practice:

→ A candidate applies. AI screens, scores, and schedules the interview. The recruiter reviews the shortlist and makes the call.

→ An offer is accepted. WorkflowFiesta triggers IT provisioning, the welcome sequence, the Slack invite, and the manager briefing — all from one event. HR shows up to the first day, not the checklist.

→ Someone resigns. Offboarding compliance runs automatically. Access revocation, equipment return, exit survey, final paycheck — all triggered, all tracked.

The ROI is concrete: 15–20 hours recovered per hire. For a company hiring 20 people a year, that's 300–400 hours of HR capacity back in the business annually.
