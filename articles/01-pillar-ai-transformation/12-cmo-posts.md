## LINKEDIN POST — Long Form

Most companies think they're doing an AI transformation.

They're not. They're doing AI experiments.

There's a difference. A big one.

Experiments: your marketing team uses ChatGPT for copy. Finance has a macro that saves an hour. Sales tried an outreach tool and got mixed results.

Transformation: those tools connect. A signal in one department triggers a coordinated response across three others. The whole business runs differently.

I've been thinking about this a lot lately. The companies that are actually winning with AI aren't the ones with the biggest budgets or the most sophisticated models. They're the ones who figured out the right sequence:

1. Pick the highest-volume manual workflow in one department
2. Map every step
3. Build the AI version
4. Measure before and after
5. Then connect it to the next workflow

That's it. No consultants. No 12-month planning process. No data science team.

The hardest part isn't the technology. It's the organizational change — getting department heads to own the transformation instead of handing it to IT.

We just published a full department-by-department guide on how to actually do this. HR, marketing, finance, sales, CS, operations — each one has a different automation profile and different ROI leverage points.
