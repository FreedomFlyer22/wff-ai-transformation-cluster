AI transformation is not a technology project.

It is an operating model change.

The businesses that get this right follow a consistent pattern: they start with the highest-volume manual workflow in one department, automate it, measure the results, and then connect it to the next workflow. Department by department. Workflow by workflow.

WorkflowFiesta published a complete guide covering what this looks like across HR, marketing, finance, sales, customer success, and operations — with specific workflows, ROI benchmarks, and a four-phase implementation roadmap.
